Understanding Well-being

A country's progress isn't just about its economy. The OECD Better Life Index measures well-being across 11 crucial dimensions – from health and housing to work-life balance and community connections.

This tool brings that rich data to life, allowing you to see the world through a lens that values what truly matters for people's lives.

Your Journey Through the Data

1. Discover Dimensions

Select what matters most to you – health, environment, safety, jobs, and more. Tailor the interactive map to focus your exploration.

2. Compare Places

See how nations stack up visually. Hover for quick scores, click to zoom in, and even compare two countries side-by-side for detailed insights.

3. Uncover Trends

Travel through time! Use the slider (2006-2023) to watch how well-being dimensions have evolved, revealing progress and challenges over the years.
